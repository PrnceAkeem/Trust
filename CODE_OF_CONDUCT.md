# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in Trust respectful, professional,
and welcoming for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their effects.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Publicly disclosing a suspected vulnerability before coordinated resolution.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional workplace.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates these standards. They
will seek a response proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Reports should be made privately to the project maintainers. A private contact
method will be added before the repository accepts outside participation. Do not
use conduct-reporting channels for vulnerability details; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as reasonably possible and
will not knowingly retaliate against a good-faith reporter.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project, including repository activity, community forums, and project events.
